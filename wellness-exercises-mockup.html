Neuroplasticity
Daily Brain Health
The most reliable way to support your brain is through consistent lifestyle habits — not games.
Move your body
Any movement counts — walk, stretch, swim, lift. Duration doesn't matter.
Learn something new
A fact, skill, chapter, or conversation outside your usual domain.
Quality sleep last night
7–9 hours for most adults. Sleep is when the brain consolidates memory.
Why these three?
Physical movement stimulates BDNF — a protein that supports neuron growth. Novel learning creates new synaptic connections. Sleep consolidates the day's learning into long-term memory. Together, these three outperform any commercial "brain training" game on the market.
